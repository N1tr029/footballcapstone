These positions are a reconstruction, not tracking data. The All-22 for this play has never been released in a form a computer can read, so every path here is hand-authored to match the shape of the play, then interpolated. That is exactly the gap this project exists to close: the same scene, drawn from positions a vision model measured off the broadcast footage instead of positions a person drew by hand.

Prototype for the replay-engine workstream — the play is stored as timed keyframes in plays/philly-special.js, in the field coordinates the five workstreams share: x 0–120 downfield, y 0–53.3 across, t in seconds from the snap. Sample it at 10 Hz and it is a TrackingFrame array. Anything that produces those frames — the vision pipeline, the play model's simulated alternative — draws through this same renderer.
