Field Calibrator

Click any point on the frame whose place on the field you can name, then say what it is in the panel. Four is the minimum. Use two different yard lines and hit each at two different distances across — four points along one line is the one arrangement that cannot work, and the panel will tell you if you get there.

The magnifier follows your cursor so you can land on the actual paint. Once four points are labelled, a green field model is drawn over the frame — if its lines sit on the real yard lines, the calibration is right.
